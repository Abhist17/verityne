VERITYNE - Deepfake-aware KYC verification
==========================================

Six independent detectors, a calibrated fusion layer, and a human-readable
explanation behind every verdict. Built on the assumption that the attacker has
Stable Diffusion and DeepFaceLab on their laptop.

What makes it unusual is not the detectors. It is that every number it states
has a committed evidence file behind it, including the numbers that are bad.


LINKS
-----

Hosted (always on):   https://verityne.onrender.com
  Full dashboard, real data. Free tier, so it sleeps - first load after a quiet
  spell takes about 50 seconds. Live scoring is off here (see NOTE below).

Live scoring (while my machine is on):
  https://arranged-homepage-voting-cow.trycloudflare.com
  Same product with the models loaded, on a GPU. Scores a packet in ~2.5s.

Repo:                 https://github.com/Abhist17/verityne

NOTE: the hosted link ships without PyTorch, which is what lets it run in
150 MB on a free 512 MB tier. Every verdict it ever computed is in the database
and reads back in full - score, reasons, all six detector breakdowns, heatmaps -
and the page labels those "stored verdict, not re-scored" rather than passing a
database read off as a fresh computation. What it cannot do is score a new
packet you upload.


THE NUMBERS, INCLUDING THE UNCOMFORTABLE ONES
---------------------------------------------

  0.753   Held-out ROC-AUC, 105 packets, identity-disjoint split.
          Was 0.913 until an ablation found the corpus leaking its own labels.

  37%     Recall at the reject threshold (0.80), precision 95%.
          The rest goes to a human rather than through.

  2.0%    False reject rate - genuine merchants blocked. The threshold is the
          argmax of the system's own cost curve, not a number picked by hand.

  1.000   Keystroke detector, held-out AUC on 17,040 real sessions
          (168,595 people from the Aalto study, plus CMU).

  0.500   The same detector under replay attack. We built the attack that
          defeats it: replay a real person's recorded timings and it is at
          chance.

  0.123   Worst third-party fake (StyleGAN), from a dataset we did not
          generate. Below 0.5 means inverted - it scores those fakes as MORE
          genuine than real faces.


WHAT WE GOT WRONG, PUBLISHED
-----------------------------

Nine beliefs this project held, measured, and lost - 4 fixed, 2 designed
around, 3 still open - each linked to the evidence file that disproved it.
The /corrections page is the one worth your time.

  FIXED  Half the headline AUC was a label we wrote into our own files. The
         corpus conditioned EXIF mode on the label. 0.913 became 0.753.

  FIXED  The identity threshold would have rejected two thirds of honest
         applicants. Fitted on our own pairs; on LFW's 6,000 real pairs it
         accepted only 52% of genuine matches.

  OPEN   The frequency head separates real Indian faces from real FFHQ faces
         at 0.916. Two groups that are BOTH genuine. It learned the prompt
         list, not synthesis.

  OPEN   The selfie detector does not work on fakes we did not generate. At or
         below chance on all four third-party generator families.


WHERE TO POKE
-------------

  Metrics  The fused ROC against each detector alone. If a single detector
           beats the fusion anywhere, the fusion weights are wrong.
           Behavioral reads as a dash, not 0.500, because it never ran on this
           corpus; the footnote says where its real number lives.

  Review   27 cases. Eleven carry a "same face as N" badge - the same
           photograph onboarding under several names. That repetition is the
           fraud, not a glitch in the data.

  Threat   The same reuse drawn as rings. One fake merchant is an incident;
           forty sharing a face is organised.

  Verify   Click a fixture. On the GPU link it scores live; on the hosted link
           it opens the stored verdict, labelled as such.


KNOWN WEAK POINTS
-----------------

Stated here so nobody has to find them by accident.

  - 0.753 is not a good AUC. The honest version is that a genuine merchant and
    a fraudulent one overlap through the middle of the distribution. The
    landing page draws that overlap rather than hiding it behind one figure.

  - The corpus is small and synthetic: 105 held-out packets. Thresholds read
    off held-out report themselves - an operating point, not a generalisation
    claim.

  - Detector 6 is defeated by replay, and the fix is not a better rhythm model.
    A replayed recording is a REUSED one, which is the linkage problem this
    repo already solves for faces and files.

  - The API is open on the hosted link and accepts uploads. Fine for a review
    link, wrong for anything else.


238 tests over the deterministic surface, including a suite that fails the
build if a number in the documentation stops matching the evidence file behind
